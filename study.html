Moon Design System

  • From fragmented libraries to one scalable system.
  • Combined strategy, system design and execution.
  • Delivered while product teams were actively shipping.

Moon Design System snapshots

1) Business Goal & Design Challenge

  • Increase speed and consistency across products.
  • Keep flexibility for different product brands/themes.
  • Replace legacy inconsistencies with a scalable token system.
  • Make the system usable in real delivery timelines.

Component structure and token strategy

2) Approaches Explored & Decision

  • Explored: one big file vs modular files vs hybrid model.
  • Selected: strong Core + modular component libraries.
  • Tested token models; chose component tokens referencing global semantics.
  • Decision criteria: maintainability, theming flexibility, dev alignment.

Process: Research, System, Tools

  • Audited components and recurring product inconsistencies.
  • Built token architecture with Figma Variables and modes.
  • Used atomic/modular patterns for reusable components.
  • Documented system in Next.js and iterated from team feedback.

Documentation and implementation process

Team Collaboration

  • Worked daily with designers, engineers and design leadership.
  • Used critiques, workshops and implementation handovers.
  • Validated decisions in active product squads.
  • Aligned system choices with engineering constraints early.

Cross-team collaboration around Moon

3) Why It Was the Right Direction

  • Adoption increased across product teams.
  • Documentation became part of day-to-day delivery.
  • Design-development communication became faster and clearer.
  • Incremental shipping reduced risk and improved quality.

Impact through reuse and consistency

Impact & Business Results

  • Stronger foundation for scalable product delivery.
  • Shared language across design and engineering.
  • Reusable tokens/components reduced duplicate work.
  • Clearer onboarding path for new products.
